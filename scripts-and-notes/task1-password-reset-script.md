# Task 1: Account Lockout & Beneficiary Update Workflow

## Client Scenario
* **Client Name:** John Smith
* **Goal:** Regain access to online banking after account lockout, and update account beneficiaries following a recent marriage.
* **Issue:** Received error message `"Password Reset Required. You’ve made too many login attempts."`

## Key Call Steps Executed
1. **De-escalation & Greeting:** Authenticated client identity, acknowledged the lockout frustration, and congratulated the client on his marriage.
2. **Active Listening:** Identified that the underlying need behind logging in was to update account elections (beneficiary status).
3. **Problem Resolution & Guidance:** 
   * Initiated the password reset protocol to restore account access (5-minute estimated handling time).
   * Guided the client on where to locate self-service beneficiary forms online once logged back in.

## Key Takeaways
* **Client Loyalty Impact:** Validating life events (like marriage) builds rapport and improves customer satisfaction.
* **Efficiency:** Combining account recovery with clear self-service guidance minimizes callback rates and reduces friction for routine account maintenance.
