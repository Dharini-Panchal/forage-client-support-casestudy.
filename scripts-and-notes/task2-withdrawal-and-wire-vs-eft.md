# Task 2: Withdrawal Request & Transfer Analysis

## Client Scenario
* **Client Name:** Jane Smith
* **Goal:** Withdraw $100,000 for a home closing next Tuesday.
* **Account Balance:** $320,000 total ($10,000 cash available, $310,000 invested across 10 stocks).

## Action Plan Executed
1. **Liquidate Investments:** Initiated trade to sell $90,000 worth of investments to meet the $100,000 target.
2. **Explain Trade Settlement:** Informed client that stock sales take 2 business days (T+2) to settle (Wednesday trade clears on Friday).
3. **Transfer Method Selection:** Evaluated EFT vs. Bank Wire.

## Recommendation Rationale
Because Jane's trade clears on Friday and her home closing is next Tuesday, **Bank Wire** is recommended. An EFT takes 1–3 business days and risks missing the deadline, whereas a Bank Wire processes same-day, providing guaranteed funds for real estate closing.
